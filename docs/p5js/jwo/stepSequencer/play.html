Load and Use

  1. Click the image and press <SPACE> to start and stop;
  2. Click 1️⃣, 2️⃣, 3️⃣, 4️⃣ or 5️⃣ on the keyboard for a preset beat and then be creative!

Be Creative!

  • S ubstitute one channel (row) for another;
  • C ombine beats from two of the different presets;
  • A dapt by changing the beat for one of the channels;
  • M odify by adding a new channel;
  • P ut to Another Use by using a channel from the person sitting next you in your beat (with permission);
  • E liminate a channel, or a column to add silence - do this for the person sitting next you (with permission);
  • R everse a channel - what happens if you
