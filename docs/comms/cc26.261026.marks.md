So - 

 * if you have commits on 3 different dates you get 15 marks (two dates 10 marks, just one day 5 marks)
 * if you have commits over a period of more than a week, then you get another 5.
 * if you have commits over a period of more than two weeks, then you get another 5.

With **25 max**.
