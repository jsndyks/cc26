> ❓**I have written an application over the summer that synthesizes and generates data - can I use this data that I am making as the basis for the Dear Data task?**

That would be a strong **NO**.

We want you to make _observations_ about the world around you and perhaps your behaviour, note these down, and (after next week's sessions) load them into _Processing_ to see how they look ... in ways that are creative and expressive and interesting ... and perhaps revealing.

So think perhaps about :
 - your contributions to the application (when, what, how significant, how challenging, how succesasful);
 - your use of the app (when, what, how did you feel?);

Both of these answers are reallty addressed by the idea of the **smaller contextual question** that is described in [Dear Data - To DRAW is to REMEMBER](https://rl.talis.com/3/city/items/4551ab37-962c-400e-aba3-5053d168fd75.html).

Asking these questions and adding answers to your observations gives you rich data sets that may enable you to make _discoveries_ when you _depict_ them.

> ❓**I am collecting data that will give me 1 data point per day. If this is not enough to collect 100 data points by the end of the Dear Data assignment, is it fine to collect data on this topic regardless? Or should I pick something that definitely can reach 100 data points?**

So, there are very few **requirements** for this task - so few that some people have hardly noticed them.

We tried to fit them on just [eight short and punchy grey backgrounded lines here](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1463045).

But they are **important** and it's [Dear Data 100](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1463045), not **Dear Data 63.5**.

They are **important** because _you get points for data points_!<br/>
Look at the **Marking Criteria**.<br/>
25% of the mark for Part 2 is awarded for data collection:

> The sketch shows 100 data items on a selected topic in light of the Dear Data 100 activity:<br/>
    **_Data - 25 Marks :   0.25 marks per item - up to 25 for 100 items_**

So you can collect fewer data points than 100, but you will get fewer marks that you would if you collected 100 data points. So I would go for 100.

And if you want to collect on a daily basis - use [SCAMPER](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394317) (see _Section 4_) - to come up with a creative way of making more than one observation per day so that you can produce 100 data points.
