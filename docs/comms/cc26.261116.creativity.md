Remember that your [_Dear Data 100_](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1463045) sketch must ...

>  depict a data set that you have collected over time<br/>
> that explores some aspect of the world<br/>
> that is interesting to you<br/>
> through at least 100 observations (data points)

So, as long as you persuade the assessors that you've done that, you're in good shape!

During the [Part 2 Check-In](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1463045) we will look to see that :

> The sketch involves some creativity - and the student is able to describe & justify this with reference to SCAMPER terminology

So you could talk about data collection, depiction, or both.<br/>
We just want _some creativity_, and _some explanation_ that shows _some knowledge_ of the concepts we've introduced to you.

If the thing that interests you is _"stuff I do to relax"_ or _"popular culture that I consume, how I feel about it, how much it costs, and what I'm eating while I'm consuming it"_ then they could be observations about films, gigs, and even basketball matches (I saw _Paris_ defeat _Fenerbahçe_ in a classic overtime buzzer-beating climax last week - and had a half decent falafel gyro on the side!) 

They need to be **your observations**, so they won't be _collected before_, and so there will be some originality, and as you know, originality and creativity are closely related.

We would encourage you to try to be creative in thinking about the data that you collect, because that's in the spirit of _Dear Data_ and this is an opportunity to experiment with some of these important creativity techniques. And perhaps find out a bit about yourself too.
