##### WHAT ?

We have received few very reasonable messages from folks that say things along the lines of _"what exactly am I supposed to be doing for this assignment then?"_.

Well - we want **a Processing sketch that depicts 100 data items that you have collected about some topic**.
See these [Requirements](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1463045)

The sessions next week on _Data_ and _Data Visualization_ and _Images_ will help lots, with examples and capability and some reassurance.
These materials will show you how to load and depict data in your sketches.

For now, there is lots to do that will help you make progress :

 - check the **3 Dear Data examples** that you select in the [Task 3 : 3/52 Weeks (SPIN & STUDY)](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394317) exercise for ideas - really look at the examples and their legends / descriptions, and the commentaries by Giorgia and Stefanie.<br/>You could discuss them on the discussion forum;<br/><br/>
 - choose a topic (you can always change it) and try do something that is inspired by a Dear Data example (usew SCAMPER) in your NoteBook, on paper, about a subject that interests you. Imagine what the data might look like if you collect and depict it;<br/><br/>
 - then collect some actual data about your topic - the [Dear Data : Part 2 - Your Project](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394317) section (see 4, 5 and 6) should help, as should the [Dear Data Collection](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394319) page. **Do read the guidance**.<br/><br/>
 - keep working on [Methods](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394334), [Loops](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394329), [Groups](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394331), [Decisions](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394326) and [Interaction](https://moodle4.city.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=1394340) materials - there is lots to learn to help you produce your sketch;<br/><br/>
 - and then **in time**, see whether you can do something based on your sketches and initial ideas (or something completely different - ideas evolve), but digital and interactive, in _Processing_?

 If your don't think you can do the _Processing_ bit yet, don't worry - working with the materials, and engaging with the sessions next week will get you there.
