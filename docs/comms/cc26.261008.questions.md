> ❓**can I record data about how angry I get when playing computer games - friends have mentioned that I can get quite worked up**

Ummm ... yeah.<br/>Go steady though!

_Dear Data_ is a chance to learn about (and adapt) behaviour.

So do think about angry episodes - what might you collect to **help you learn about** your anger: what causes it, how it is expressed, the impact it may have on others?

 - The time it happened
 - What game was it? What level?<br/>(_do games still have levels?_)
 - Where were you?
 - How angry were you?
 - How did you respond?
 - What actually caused the anger?
 - Who were you with?
 - What did they do?
 - etc.

Hopefully you can use this process to find out a bit about yourself and decide how and who you want to be.
