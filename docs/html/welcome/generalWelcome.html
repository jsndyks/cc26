The #CreativeCoding activity is a term-long experience in code, data collection, colours on canvas and core concepts from Computer Science. We expect it to be of long-term benefit!

It runs as an introduction to Java Programming and is designed for both completely new and experienced programmers.
We will start from the very basics of programming and quickly develop capabilities for creating expressive content including digital art and data visualization.

Lectures and associated practical classes take place throughout term as shown on MyTimeTable.

Remember your Fib Friend Badge. There are many different versions. Your mission is to find as many others as you can with the same design as yours. Collect their names (in your notebook) and share something meaningful about yourself with them ... and talk about University, #CreativeCoding and fun stuff.

In #CreativeCoding we will essentially learn Java, and this is a great introduction to the language and its concepts. But we will rely on an environment called Processing – that will introduce key programming concepts and enable you to create interactive, graphical, animated applications with much less effort than regular Java.

#CreativeCoding is assessed through two assignments, that together will account for 50% of your Term 1 mark for Java Programming.

Important: we plan to hit the ground running.

  • We expect you to bring charged laptops to lectures with Processing installed - you will be coding.
  • We expect you to have completed the preparation beforehand - the #CreativeCoding Preparation Activities, the Dear Data work and the GitHub setup are essential preparation.
