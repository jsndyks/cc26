can i cook while recording my data?
3
I don't expect a full risk assessment before each observation is made in recorded, but do be sure to be safe and not put yourself or others at risk when collecting data. Taking photos while cycling, taking photos of hazard lines in the middle of roads while traffic is passing, climbing large trees to collect the tallest leaf of each impressive [Platanus hispanica](https://apps.london.gov.uk/public-realm-trees/explore?borough=E09000019) that you pass, are examples of risky data collection that you should avoid.
So please be sensible adults and be sure to act with caution and safety in this task, and life generally.

Is it OK if I create my graphs as images and then load the images using the sketch like PowerPoint?
-24
I can think of ways in which this could be expressive, but I also think there's a danger that what do you produce would be very routine and that this approach might not involve you engaging with the kind of learning and coding that we're trying to encourage and support you into. _Processing_, and the features that we've introduced, are designed to explicitly support people introducing exactly the kind of thing we're asking for here... Which is exactly the kind of activity and output that's employers are asking computer science graduates to be able to do.
So while I can feel positive about creative approaches to problem-solving (?) I worry a little if people are not entering into the spirit of the activity. And if you end up making graphs in excel and loading an image into _Processing_, you're not learning much, and you're not going to impress the assesses. This would feel like a totally wasted opportunity for learning at university.
